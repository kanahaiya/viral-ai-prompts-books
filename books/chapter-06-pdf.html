GUIDE

How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised product photography image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [SQUARE BRACKETS] — such as [PRODUCT] [TYPE] [MATERIAL] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[PRODUCT]Product name or brand
[TYPE]Product category or type
[MATERIAL]Primary material or finish
[BACKGROUND]Background or surface
[LIGHTING]Lighting style
[ANGLE]Camera angle or framing
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
A commercial product photograph of [PRODUCT], a [TYPE] made of [MATERIAL], positioned on [BACKGROUND]. The product is centered and perfectly in focus. [LIGHTING] eliminates harsh shadows. Shot [ANGLE].
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
A commercial product photograph of LUMINA Serum, a skincare bottle made of dark amber glass, positioned on pure white background. The product is centered and perfectly in focus. Soft diffused studio lighting eliminates harsh shadows. Shot 45-degree hero angle.

How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised professional headshot image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [SQUARE BRACKETS] — such as [NAME] [TITLE] [APPEARANCE] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[NAME]Your full name
[TITLE]Your job title or role
[APPEARANCE]Brief physical description
[OUTFIT]Professional attire
[EXPRESSION]Desired expression/mood
[BACKGROUND]Background or setting
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
A photorealistic professional headshot of [NAME], [TITLE]. [APPEARANCE], wearing [OUTFIT]. [EXPRESSION] expression communicating executive confidence. Shot on an 85mm lens, soft studio lighting, [BACKGROUND].
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
A photorealistic professional headshot of Sarah Chen, Marketing Director. 35-year-old woman, shoulder-length dark hair, wearing tailored navy blazer. Confident and approachable expression communicating executive confidence. Shot on an 85mm lens, soft studio lighting, clean white studio.

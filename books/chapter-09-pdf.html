How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised pet transformation portrait image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [SQUARE BRACKETS] — such as [PET NAME] [BREED] [COAT] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[PET NAME]Your pet's name
[BREED]Breed or species
[COAT]Coat colour and texture
[THEME]Creative transformation theme
[PROP]Prop or accessory
[SETTING]Background or location
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
A regal oil painting portrait of [PET NAME] the [BREED] with [COAT] fur, dressed as [THEME], wearing a rich velvet coat with gold brocade trim and a lace cravat, one paw resting on [PROP]. Seated in [SETTING] with an aristocratic air.
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
A regal oil painting portrait of LUNA the golden retriever with fluffy golden fur, dressed as a Victorian aristocrat, wearing a rich velvet coat with gold brocade trim and a lace cravat, one paw resting on a tiny brass telescope. Seated in an ornate royal library with an aristocratic air.

How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised childhood nostalgia portrait image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [SQUARE BRACKETS] — such as [NAME] [AGE] [HAIR] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[NAME]Child's first name (in capitals)
[AGE]Age description of the child
[HAIR]Hair colour and style
[ERA]Decade or era for the aesthetic
[ACTIVITY]What the child is doing
[SETTING]Location or environment
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
A photorealistic portrait of [NAME], a [AGE] with [HAIR], [ACTIVITY] in [SETTING]. Warm golden-hour lighting, soft film grain, captured as if on a beloved family camera.
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
A photorealistic portrait of EMMA, a 6-year-old with wild curly red hair, picking wildflowers in a sunlit meadow. Warm golden-hour lighting, soft film grain, captured as if on a beloved family camera.

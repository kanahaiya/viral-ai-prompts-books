How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised cinematic movie poster image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [SQUARE BRACKETS] — such as [NAME] [ROLE] [OUTFIT] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[NAME]Character or actor name
[ROLE]Character's role or archetype
[OUTFIT]Character's costume
[GENRE]Film genre
[PALETTE]Colour palette & mood
[TAGLINE]Film tagline text
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
A cinematic movie poster for a [GENRE] film: [NAME] as [ROLE] in [OUTFIT] stands with back to the viewer on a rain-soaked neon-lit city street at 2am. [PALETTE] with neon reflected in wet pavement. Tagline: '[TAGLINE]'.
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
A cinematic movie poster for a noir thriller film: Alex Morgan as a rogue spy in a tailored black suit stands with back to the viewer on a rain-soaked neon-lit city street at 2am. Dark and moody, deep blues and crimson with neon reflected in wet pavement. Tagline: 'Trust no one. Run.'.

CH 02 Anatomy of a Perfect Prompt — The 6 Building Blocks

Every stunning AI image is built from the same 6 ingredients. Master these and you'll never write a weak prompt again.

01 · SUBJECT
Who or What
The main focus. Be specific — age, gender, ethnicity, expression, clothing, object type, material.
e.g. "a 25-year-old Indian woman in a red saree"
"a matte black ceramic coffee mug"
02 · STYLE
Art or Photo Style
The visual language. Defines whether it looks like a photo, painting, illustration, or film frame.
e.g. "Studio Ghibli animation"
"cinematic photography, film grain"
03 · LIGHTING
Light Source & Quality
Lighting is everything. It determines mood, depth, and whether the image looks professional or flat.
e.g. "golden hour, warm side lighting"
"dramatic studio rim lighting, soft diffused"
04 · MOOD
Atmosphere & Emotion
Tells the AI the emotional quality to aim for. Separates forgettable from unforgettable images.
e.g. "ethereal and dreamlike"
"dark, moody, tension-filled"
"warm, joyful, nostalgic"
05 · CAMERA
Lens, Angle & Shot Type
Specific camera language instantly elevates image realism and composition quality.
e.g. "85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field"
"drone overhead wide angle, Sony A7R IV"
06 · QUALITY
Technical Quality Boosters
Signal the quality level. These words activate the AI's highest detail modes.
e.g. "photorealistic, 8K, award-winning"
"highly detailed, professional photography"
Subject + Style + Lighting + Mood + Camera + Quality = STUNNING IMAGE
The 11 prompt books do this for you automatically. Every template already has all 6 building blocks pre-wired. Your job is just to fill in the variables — the formula is built in.
CH 03 8 Common Mistakes That Kill Image Quality
❌

Mistake 1 — Being Too Vague

"A woman" tells the AI almost nothing. It has to guess everything — age, ethnicity, expression, clothing, setting. Each guess averages out to something generic.

✓ FIX: "A 28-year-old Indian woman, confident expression, silk kurta, warm skin tone"
❌

Mistake 2 — Forgetting Lighting

Lighting is the single biggest factor in whether an image looks amateur or professional. Flat, unspecified lighting makes every image look like a smartphone snapshot.

✓ FIX: Always add one of — golden hour / studio rim lighting / dramatic side light / soft diffused / neon glow
❌

Mistake 3 — No Camera Specification

Without camera language, the AI defaults to a bland, neutral composition. Specifying lens and shot type transforms the image into something that looks genuinely professional.

✓ FIX: Add "85mm portrait lens, shallow bokeh" or "wide angle, drone overhead" to any prompt
❌

Mistake 4 — Conflicting Styles

"Photorealistic cartoon" or "impressionist photograph" confuses the AI and produces muddy, inconsistent results. Pick one lane and stay in it.

✓ FIX: Choose either photorealism OR an illustration style — never both at the same time
❌

Mistake 5 — Too Many Elements

Listing 10+ things in one prompt overwhelms the AI. It tries to fit everything in and produces a cluttered, unfocused composition where nothing looks good.

✓ FIX: One hero subject, one background, 2–3 supporting details. Less is more precise.
❌

Mistake 6 — No Mood or Atmosphere

Without a mood directive, images come out emotionally neutral. They look technically correct but feel empty — like a stock photo nobody asked for.

✓ FIX: Add one mood word — ethereal / melancholic / vibrant / mysterious / warm and nostalgic
SP Spacer
❌

Mistake 7 — Using the Wrong Tool

Using ChatGPT for highly stylized art, or Midjourney when you need text inside the image — wrong tool for the job always produces disappointing results.

✓ FIX: Use the tool comparison in Chapter 1. Match your goal to the right platform first.
❌

Mistake 8 — Ignoring Aspect Ratio

Generating a portrait-style image in landscape ratio (or vice versa) wastes half the frame and forces awkward composition. Always set ratio to match your use case.

✓ FIX: Midjourney: --ar 9:16 (portrait), --ar 16:9 (landscape), --ar 1:1 (square)

CH 07 Platform-Specific Tips — Get the Best from Each Tool
Midjourney
Discord
  • Use --ar 9:16 for portrait, --ar 16:9 for landscape
  • Add --v 6.1 for latest model (most photorealistic)
  • Use --style raw to reduce over-stylization
  • Add --q 2 for highest quality (slower)
  • Upscale your favorite: click U1–U4 below the image grid
  • Use /describe on any image to reverse-engineer its prompt
ChatGPT (DALL·E 3)
Web / App
  • Paste prompt and say "generate this image" — no special commands needed
  • Use conversational editing: "make her expression warmer"
  • Say "keep everything the same but change the lighting to golden hour"
  • Ask for "a 9:16 portrait orientation" directly in text
  • DALL·E excels at following detailed instructions precisely — more so than Midjourney
  • Weakest for highly stylized art — use Midjourney for those
Adobe Firefly
Creative Cloud
  • Best choice for commercial use — fully copyright-safe images
  • Add Content Type: Photo in settings for realism
  • Use the Structure Reference feature to match your composition
  • The Style Reference feature lets you match any image's aesthetic
  • Integrates directly into Photoshop's Generative Fill — best for editing
  • Use --negative prompt field to exclude unwanted elements
Ideogram
Web
  • Best tool for text inside images — logos, posters, signs, labels
  • Add "with text [YOUR TEXT]" anywhere in the prompt
  • Use Magic Prompt: ON for automatic prompt enhancement
  • Select Design mode for typography-heavy work (posters, thumbnails)
  • Free tier gives 10 slow generations/day — enough for testing
  • Weakest for photo-realistic faces — use ChatGPT or Midjourney for those

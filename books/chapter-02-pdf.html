GUIDE

How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised Ghibli & anime image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the [TOKENS] with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in [BRACKETS] — such as [NAME], [WORLD], [OUTFIT] etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 anime style categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every [TOKEN] with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT (with your photo attached), Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
[NAME]Your character's anime name
[WORLD]Ghibli film or anime universe
[OUTFIT]What your character wears
[COMPANION]Animal or spirit beside you
[SETTING]Scene or location
[TIME]Time of day or mood
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from scratch using text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
Reimagine this photo in the art style of Studio Ghibli's "[WORLD]". Transform [NAME] into an anime character with large expressive eyes, soft watercolour skin tones, wearing [OUTFIT]. Place them in [SETTING] at [TIME], [COMPANION] glowing softly beside them. Art direction by Hayao Miyazaki. Painted cel-shading with rich jewel-tone colours and soft ink outlines.
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
Reimagine this photo in the art style of Studio Ghibli's "Spirited Away". Transform Sakura into an anime character with large expressive eyes, soft watercolour skin tones, wearing red summer kimono. Place them in spirit bathhouse, misty corridors at golden sunset, small white fox spirit glowing softly beside them. Art direction by Hayao Miyazaki. Painted cel-shading with rich jewel-tone colours and soft ink outlines.

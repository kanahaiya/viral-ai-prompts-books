GUIDE

How to Use This Prompt Book

100 personalised action figure & toy box image prompts — each with 6 variable slots. Replace the {TOKENS} with your details and paste into any AI image tool.
How to Use — 4 Steps
1
Each prompt contains 6 personal variables shown in {CURLY BRACES} — such as {NAME}, {ROLE}, {OUTFIT} etc. Replace each with your own details before using.
2
Browse the 100 prompts grouped into 5 action figure style categories. Find one that matches the look you want to create — each is a complete image generation prompt.
3
Copy the prompt text and replace every {TOKEN} with your personal detail. See the example below — it shows a blank template next to the same prompt fully filled in.
4
Paste the completed prompt into ChatGPT (with your photo attached), Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, or DALL·E 3. The preamble at the top helps the AI understand each variable before it reads the creative prompt.
Your 6 Variables — What Each Means
{NAME}Your name on the toy box
{ROLE}Job title or character role
{DESCRIPTION}Brief physical description
{OUTFIT}Clothing or costume
{ACCESSORIES}Props the figure holds
{BOX_COLOR}Toy box color scheme
No photo needed for some tools. Midjourney and Firefly generate images from scratch using text alone. For ChatGPT, upload your photo alongside the prompt for best results.
Tip: The preamble at the start of each prompt (the variable definitions) helps the AI understand your values before reading the creative prompt — always keep it when copying.
Example — Prompt #1 — Template vs Filled-In
Template (unfilled)
Transform this photo into a hyper-realistic 3D collectible action figure of {DESCRIPTION} working as {ROLE}, wearing {OUTFIT}, packaged in a premium retail toy box with a {BOX_COLOR} metallic color scheme. The figure holds {ACCESSORIES} through the clear window. The box reads "{NAME} — Career Collection | Limited Edition."
Filled-in example (tokens replaced with your details)
Transform this photo into a hyper-realistic 3D collectible action figure of woman in her 30s, shoulder-length black hair working as Senior Product Designer, wearing tailored navy blazer and white shirt, packaged in a premium retail toy box with a midnight blue and gold metallic color scheme. The figure holds a laptop and coffee mug through the clear window. The box reads "Sarah Chen — Career Collection | Limited Edition."
